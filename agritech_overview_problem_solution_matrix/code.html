01 Erratic 3-Phase Power at Midnight & Remote Pump Access
SUB-GHZ RF / HARDWARE CLASS A
The Farmer Problem

State electricity dispatch delivers 3-phase power between 11 PM and 4 AM. Farmers risk animal bites, falls, and motor burnout if a single phase drops unnoticed while running unattended.

Tools Digital Hardware

TD-RAD12 Sub-GHz Long-Range Radio Remote Pump Controller

Industrial 415V DIN-rail contactor adapter with integrated physical piezo ACK.
Operating Architecture

Sub-GHz frequency hop packet transceiver connects directly to the panel starter. Handheld field unit transmits 12 km deterministic pulses. Handheld emits high-decibel beep when pump switch confirms contact.

Concrete Real-World Result
100% Night Hazards Avoided

Saves 1.5–2 hours of nightly travel across broken terrain; preserves motor health against unphased brownouts; zero SIM recharge fees.

02 Night-time Crop Devastation by Wild Boars, Deer & Nilgai
ACOUSTIC SWEEP / SOLAR HARVESTING
The Farmer Problem

Single nights of wild boar root-digging ruin up to ₹60,000 worth of harvestable sugarcane, peanuts, or young maize. Fixed sirens fail after 3 nights as herds habituate.

Tools Digital Hardware

TD-DET800 Non-Habituating Ultrasonic/Acoustic Field Sentry

Monocrystalline solar-to-supercapacitor pole mount with anti-tamper enclosure.
Operating Architecture

Microcontroller executes pseudo-random frequency sweeps (16 kHz – 28 kHz) paired with asynchronous high-lux xenon strobe bursts. Prevents neuro-acoustic tolerance in wild animals.

Concrete Real-World Result
>85% Crop Incursion Cut

Documented reduction in perimeter foraging with zero ecological harm or legal liabilities associated with unauthorized lethal wire fences.

03 Over-Irrigation, Root Rot & Aquifer Depletion
CLOSED-LOOP AUTONOMOUS RELAY
The Farmer Problem

Farmers leave pumps running blindly for entire power windows out of fear of water shortages, leading to fungal collar rot in horti-trees and massive underground water waste.

Tools Digital Hardware

TD-SOIL-LOOP Autonomous Moisture Contactor System

Direct opto-isolated dry contact line linking soil probes to motor switchgear.
Operating Architecture

Dual-depth corrosion-resistant stainless probes sense volumetric water content (VWC) at 15cm and 45cm. Opto-isolated relay trips pump off when active feeder root saturation reaches preset band.

Concrete Real-World Result
35% Groundwater Preserved

Eliminates drowned root zones; reduces monthly kWh power pump consumption while guaranteeing constant soil oxygenation.

04 Slow, Inaccessible Soil Testing & Guesswork Fertilization
ON-FIELD SPECTRO-IMPEDANCE
The Farmer Problem

District soil testing labs take 18–30 days to return paper reports. Farmers sow in the dark, routinely over-applying expensive chemical Urea and DAP, degrading soil biology.

Tools Digital Hardware

TD-NPK-DIGIT Rugged 5-Pin Handheld Soil Analyzer

High-visibility backlit industrial LCD display; works completely without internet.
Operating Architecture

Solid 316 stainless sensor pins execute multi-frequency electrical impedance and optical reflection spectrometry to compute N, P, K, pH, and electrical conductivity within 12 seconds on-screen.

Concrete Real-World Result
₹14,500 Saved / Acre / Season

Replaces chemical over-application with exact dosed macro-nutrients. Instant guidance in local language directly on the LCD panel.

05 Open-Pasture Grazing, Herd Wandering & Cattle Theft
SUB-GHZ GEOFENCE BEACON
The Farmer Problem

Cattle, buffaloes, and sheep herds graze on open commons and river valleys where there is no 4G signal. Straying animals damage neighbor crops, sparking village conflicts or total theft loss.

TD-PASTURE-TAG Solar Powered Cattle Ear-Tag + Base Hub

Ultra-light 28g flexible polyurethane clamp with 5-year battery reserve.
Operating Architecture

Beacon broadcasts long-range chirp every 90 seconds. If a tagged animal moves beyond the calibrated 3 km pasture perimeter, the homestead base station sounds an audible siren bell.

Concrete Real-World Result

Continuous real-time presence verification without paying recurring telecom tracking monthly fees or depending on cellular towers.
