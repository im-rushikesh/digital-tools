Article 1.0 FOUNDATIONAL ARCHITECTURE

Overview and Core Philosophy

Digital Tools Pvt. Ltd. develops software under the strict engineering mandate that personal data which is never collected cannot be leaked, compromised, subpoenaed, or monetized. Sudoku Master Pro is conceived and implemented as a self-contained, deterministic algorithmic puzzle generator that functions without cloud connectivity.

By downloading, installing, or executing Sudoku Master Pro (package identifier: com.numbers_game.protoos), you operate an isolated execution environment on your Android device. The app requires zero user identity assertions and assumes absolute user anonymity at all times.

Article 2.0 PERSISTENCE & ERASURE

On-Device Storage & Local Data Mechanics

The application stores a minimal footprint of operational game state data locally on the host device. This data comprises solely:

  • Current 9x9 numerical cell allocations, candidate pencil markings, and undo/redo stacks.
  • Cumulative gameplay statistics (total puzzles completed, win percentage, personal speedrun records).
  • User presentation settings (Dark/Light display theme, audio chime toggle, highlight duplicates toggle).
Data Removal Mechanism:

This data resides within the private app sandbox (SharedPreferences). Uninstalling the application or selecting "Clear Storage" in the Android OS settings irrevocably removes all data from the device immediately. No remote backups or lingering artifacts exist.

Article 3.0 DEPENDENCY INTEGRITY

Third-Party Libraries & External SDKs

Sudoku Master Pro does not link or embed commercial SDKs designed for tracking, monetizing, or evaluating user conduct. Specifically:

NO AD NETWORKS Zero AdMob, Unity, AppLovin, ironSource, Vungle.
NO ANALYTICS DAEMONS Zero Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Adjust, AppsFlyer.
NO REMOTE LOGGERS Zero Sentry, Datadog, or external Crashlytics services.
NO USER PROFILERS Zero Advertising ID (AAID) queries or cross-app fingerprinting.

Only standard Android Jetpack and Jetpack Compose runtime libraries are utilized for UI rendering and local mathematical board validation.

Article 4.0 CHILDREN & FAMILIES STATUTE

COPPA & Google Play Families Policy Compliance

Sudoku Master Pro is certified compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Article 8 regarding minors, and the strict guidelines set forth by the Google Play Families Policy program.

  • done The app does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • done The app contains zero advertisements, completely eliminating exposure to age-inappropriate marketing, contextual targeting, or behavioral retargeting.
  • done No social networking features, chat functions, or user-to-user communications exist in the codebase.
