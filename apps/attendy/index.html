SEC 01

Introduction & Scope of Service

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and statutory disclosure protocols of the mobile software application Attendy: Attendance & Payroll (hereinafter "Attendy", "Application", or "the System"), designed, maintained, and operated by Digital Tools Pvt. Ltd. ("Digital Tools", "we", "us", or "our").

Attendy functions as a purpose-built B2B workforce productivity, shift-scheduling, and internal enterprise payroll computation suite. This policy establishes the binding contract between Digital Tools and all legal entities, enterprise accounts, employer administrators ("Employers"), and individual personnel ("Employees" or "Users") who install or interact with the Application via the Google Play Store or authorized enterprise sideload packages.

By downloading, installing, or initializing Attendy, you acknowledge and agree to the deterministic data management mechanisms described herein. If you or your organization do not agree with the terms set forth within this document, you must discontinue use immediately and execute the removal procedures outlined in Section 7 (User Rights & Erasure).

SEC 02

Data Categories & Collection Mechanics

To uphold the foundational principle of Data Minimization under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (India 2023) and GDPR Article 5(1)(c), Attendy bifurcates data ingestion into two strict operational categories:

admin_panel_settings Employer Administrator Data
  • • Corporate registered entity name and GSTIN/Tax ID (where applicable)
  • • Administrative contact email and verification telephone number
  • • Workplace physical coordinates (Geofence latitudinal/longitudinal perimeter)
  • • Organizational shift schedules, compensation structures, and tax rules
person Employee / Worker User Data
  • • Worker legal full name, employee number/roster designation
  • • Cellular telephone number (used for verification OTP and shift sync)
  • • Timestamped punch-in and punch-out telemetry coordinates
  • • Optional photographic avatar or badge scan for identity verification
  • • Accrued work hours, overtime multiplier, salary deductions, net wages

Data Controller vs Data Processor: For all employee data submitted to Attendy by an enterprise organization, the Employer acts as the Data Controller, determining the business purpose of employee attendance management. Digital Tools serves strictly as the Data Processor, maintaining deterministic computational records on behalf of the registered Employer.

SEC 03

Android Device Permissions Justification

In strict compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policy regarding Sensitive Device Permissions, Attendy requests only the absolute minimum hardware and OS access parameters required for primary runtime operations:

my_location ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
FOREGROUND EXECUTION ONLY

Statutory Operational Purpose: Attendy requires hardware coordinate access solely to execute real-time boundary verification against employer-defined office or work-site geofences. This ensures shift punch events originate from authorized physical workplaces.

check_circle NO BACKGROUND TRACKING: Attendy NEVER utilizes ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION. Location radios are turned off immediately when punch verification completes.

Attendy uses the modern Android Photo Picker and never indexes unselected user photo libraries.

SEC 04

Data Storage, Encryption & Infrastructure Security

Attendy maintains an industrial-grade cryptographic baseline. All telemetry data and financial payroll records are managed under rigorous physical, architectural, and algorithmic controls:

Transit Encryption TLS 1.3 Strict

All client-to-cloud API exchanges use mandatory TLS 1.3 cryptographic transport with HSTS enforcement.

Rest Encryption AES-256 GCM

Employee records, hourly wage numbers, and punch geolocations are persisted using FIPS 140-2 validated AES-256.

Tenant Boundary Multi-Tenant RLS

Row-Level Security (RLS) guarantees absolute cryptographic isolation between enterprise employer domains.

Local device storage on the employee's handset utilizes Android Keystore-backed encrypted SQLite databases. If a mobile device is rooted or modified, Attendy implements runtime tamper detection to safeguard local biometric keys and encrypted salary slips.

SEC 05

Data Retention & Automated Deletion Protocols

Digital Tools retains employee and administrative workforce data solely for the duration required to deliver legitimate payroll and attendance accounting services:

  • Active Accounts: Attendance punch logs and generated payslip ledgers are retained indefinitely while an employer's commercial account remains active, facilitating mandatory multi-year tax compliance auditing.
  • Account Decommissioning: When an employer or organization requests account cancellation, all linked employee profiles, coordinate logs, and financial tables undergo a scheduled hard-deletion purge within 30 calendar days across all hot-storage and backup volumes.
  • De-Identified Crash Telemetry: Low-level OS exception stack traces and performance metrics are systematically scrubbed of IP addresses and automatically cycled on a 90-day rolling TTL.
SEC 06

Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA / GDPR Art. 8)

Attendy is strictly an enterprise B2B workforce administration utility designed for registered corporate personnel, small business employers, and legal age employees. The application is not directed to, marketed towards, or designed for children under the age of 16 (or under 13 in relevant jurisdictions).

Digital Tools does not intentionally or knowingly collect, store, or process personal identifiable data of minors. If an employer inadvertently registers an individual who does not meet statutory minimum working age requirements under regional labor laws, our Grievance Desk will immediately purge such data upon verification.
