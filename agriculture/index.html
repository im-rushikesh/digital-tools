Kisan Ki Samasya vs. Digital Tools Ka Hal

Khet me aamtaur par aane wali 4 badi pareshaniyan aur hamara hardware unhe bina internet ke kaise jad se khatam karta hai:

nightlight SAMASYA #1: RAAT ME 2 KM KA SAFAR

Raat 2 baje bijli aane par khet jaana aur zehrile saamp/keedo ka khatra

Gaon me 3-phase bijli ka koi time nahi hota. Raat ko 1 baje ya 3 baje aati hai. Kisan ko andhere me khet chal kar jaana padta hai jisse accidents aur saamp ke kaatne ka sabse bada khatra rehta hai.

check_circle DIGITAL TOOLS KA HAL: TD-RF12K

Ghar ke aangan me baithe-baithe handheld remote ka button dabaiye. 12 km door borewell starter on ho jayega aur remote buzzer bajakar bata dega ki motor ghoom gayi hai!

electric_bolt SAMASYA #2: SINGLE-PHASING SE MOTOR JALNA

Ek phase udd jane par 5 minute me motor ka coil jal jana (₹12,000 nuksan)

Rural transformers me load imbalance se aksar 1 phase gayab ho jata hai. Ordinary starter motor ko 2 phase par chalata rehta hai jisse winding garam hoke jal jaati hai aur rewinding me ₹10,000 se ₹15,000 lag jate hain.

check_circle DIGITAL TOOLS KA HAL: PHASE-VOLT TRIP

Hamara unit continuous 3-phase line sense karta hai. Agar ek bhi phase me 15% se jyada voltage drop hua, toh 400 millisecond ke andar contactor cut-off karke motor ko 100% surakshit kar deta hai.

water_damage SAMASYA #3: DRY RUN (PAANI KHATAM HONA)

Borewell me paani sookhne ke baad bhi pump ka khali ghoomna aur stage kharab hona

Garmiyo me borewell ka water table jaldi girta hai. Pump khali chalta rehta hai jisse submersible pump ke teflon bush aur stage friction se pighal jaate hain.

check_circle DIGITAL TOOLS KA HAL: CURRENT DELTA TRIP

Paani na hone par motor ka load achanak 14A se girkar 7A ho jata hai. Controller is current drop ko turant pehchan kar motor ko 12 second me shut-off kar deta hai aur dry-run timer shuru kar deta hai.

pest_control SAMASYA #4: JANGLI SUAR AUR ROJ (NILGAI)

Kadi mehnat se ugi fasal ko raat me janwaro dwara barbaad kiya jana

Ganne, makka, kela aur chane ki fasal me raat ko suar aur nilgai ghus kar lakho ka nuksan karte hain. Bijli ke taar lagana kanoonan apradh hai aur jaanleva saabit hota hai.

check_circle DIGITAL TOOLS KA HAL: TD-DETER800

Solar acoustic sound shield pseudorandom frequencies aur night strobe emit karta hai. Janwaro ko dard ya chot pahunchaye bina khet ki boundary se 250m door bhaga deta hai.
