Deploying TD-AUTOPUMP Soil Probes at Root Depths

water_drop Capacitive NPK & Moisture
1

Dual-depth placement: Insert shallow sensor at 15cm (surface evapotranspiration zone) and deep sensor at 45cm root core.

2

Lateral drip offset: Position sensor strictly 20cm away from drip emitter points to avoid false localized saturations.

3

Eliminate air pockets: Make a fine soil-water slurry and pour into probe cavity before gently pressing stainless prongs.

4

Protect conduit cables: Bury the UV-shielded signal conduit 10cm beneath plowing depth to safeguard against tilling.

DIAGNOSTIC MATRIX & ERROR CODES

Quick Field Troubleshooting Guide

Identify error symptoms without opening starter cabinets. Most telemetry anomalies can be solved on-site within 2 minutes.

Observed Symptom / LED System Root Cause Recommended Field Fix Action
Double beep + Solid Red LED Code: ERR-VOLT-03
Pump starter phase trip, reverse phase sequence, or severe single-phasing on line. Verify 3-phase grid voltage with line indicator; interchange R & B phases if auto-phase reverser trips.
RF range drops below 8 km Code: ERR-SIG-LOW
Antenna obstructed by corrugated iron shed cladding or sugarcane canopy cresting. Elevate antenna mast >1.5m clear above metallic rooflines; tighten RG58 coax connector.
Moisture probe reads exactly 0% Code: ERR-SOIL-AIR
Air pocket around stainless steel sensor prongs or loose connection at junction gland. Re-pack probe cavity using moist slurry to ensure 100% soil skin contact along prongs.
Controller clicks but starter won't pull Code: ERR-RELAY-DRV
Auxiliary contactor coil operating voltage under 280V AC (low control line transformer tap). Shift starter coil tapping to 380V or inspect starter thermal overload relay reset lever.
